Seeing Through the Tunnel.

A 2-tier Split-Edge architecture designed to accurately classify malicious network traffic (Botnets, DoS, Exploits) hidden inside encrypted TLS 1.2/1.3 tunnels, relying solely on handshake metadata and flow geometry.

Network Datasets Survey

Highlighted the pervasive limitations, artificial routing artifacts, and severe class imbalances present in public datasets, guiding our rigorous data cleansing approach.

Extractor Comparison

Proved that no single tool is sufficient. Demonstrated that semantic extractors (Zeek) and statistical extractors (NFStream) must be combined for modern NIDS.

The Dataset Dilemma: Fixing CIC-IDS2017

After evaluating alternatives like Statesphere IoT, we selected CIC-IDS2017. However, the pre-processed CSVs provided by the researchers were critically flawed due to bugs in their extraction tool, CICFlowMeter.

The CICFlowMeter Collapse

Roughly 25% of all pre-extracted flows contained meaningless data: negative flow durations, infinite byte rates, and corrupted TCP states. Training a model on this would result in a mathematically invalid system.

Our Solution: We discarded their CSVs entirely. We parsed the raw .pcap files through our own custom extraction pipeline. During training, we actively dropped highly correlated, dataset-specific routing artifacts to prevent overfitting: Fwd Header Length.1, Source IP, Destination IP, and Timestamp. This ensures the model learns the physics of an attack, not just the IP address of the attacker.

Module 1: The Hybrid Extractor

Because no single tool met our needs, we built a hybrid engine combining the mathematical geometry of NFStream with the event-driven deep inspection of Zeek.

NFStream: The Flow Geometry

Calculates packet inter-arrival times (PIAT), standard deviations of packet sizes, and enforces strict connection boundaries to catch "Low & Slow" threats.

streamer = NFStreamer(
    source=self.pcap_path,
    statistical_analysis=True,  
    # Captures sequence of lengths/times for the first 10 packets 
    # to fingerprint the handshake geometry
    splt_analysis=10,
    
    # Cuts flows that stay idle maliciously (Slowloris)
    idle_timeout=300,
    
    # Forces evaluation of long-running connections
    active_timeout=1800
)

Zeek: The Event Analyst

A custom ml_extractor.zeek script listens to specific application-layer events to extract the unencrypted metadata envelope before the payload is secured.

  • ssl_client_hello: Grabs SNI and Cipher Suites. Crucial for detecting exploits like Heartbleed during negotiation.
  • ssh_client_version & auth_failed: Logs explicit brute force attempts.
  • connection_state_remove: Logs the exact TCP tear-down state, even if the connection is maliciously aborted via RST to avoid detection.

The Merge Strategy (Avoid Cartesian Products)

Merging these datasets is computationally complex. We utilized a 5-Tuple merge (Source/Dest IP, Source/Dest Port, Protocol) aligned with a Timestamp Rolling Window (e.g., aggregating SSH flows past 60s). This prevents data duplication and allows the model to "remember" distributed brute-force attempts spanning multiple parallel connections.

The Architecture in Action

Our modules operate in a 2-tier pipeline. Tier 1 (Logistic Regression) acts as a high-speed bouncer at the edge, relying purely on lightweight flow statistics to filter out normal traffic. Flagged anomalies are sent to Tier 2 (XGBoost) at the gateway for deep, multi-class threat analysis using the heavy Zeek metadata.
